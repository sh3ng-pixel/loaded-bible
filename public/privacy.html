Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Loaded Bible ("we") helps you study the Bible and mark up your own paper Bible. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name, or the basic profile (name, picture, ID) shared by Google or TikTok when you sign in with them.
  • Your study content: highlights, notes, checklists, journal entries, tabs, insert cards, reading progress and settings you create in the app.
  • Guest mode: if you use the app without an account, everything stays only in your browser on your device.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and save your progress so it's there on all your devices.
  • When you tap a ✨ feature, the Bible reference and your request are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to write the answer. We don't send your email.

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and storage), Cloudflare (website hosting), Anthropic (AI answers), TikTok and Google (only if you choose to sign in with them), PayPal (donations — we never see your card details).

We don't sell your data or show ads.

Your choices

You can export your notes (My account → Back up), and delete your account and all its data at any time (My account → Delete account).
